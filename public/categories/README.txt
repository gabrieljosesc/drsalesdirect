Category images
===============

Drop a photo here named exactly <category-slug>.jpg and it will automatically
replace the gradient placeholder on:
  - the homepage "Shop by Category" cards
  - the category page banner header (/shop/<slug>)

Recommended: landscape, at least 1200x900px, JPG or WebP (rename to .jpg).

Homepage featured category slugs (8 cards):
  dermal-fillers.jpg
  botulinum-toxins.jpg
  skincare.jpg
  weight-loss.jpg
  peels-and-masks.jpg
  threads.jpg
  orthopedic-injections.jpg
  cannulas-and-needles.jpg

Any category page can also have a banner image, e.g.:
  ophthalmology.jpg
  gynecology.jpg
  rheumatology.jpg
  mesotherapy.jpg
  ... (use the slug shown in the URL /shop/<slug>)
