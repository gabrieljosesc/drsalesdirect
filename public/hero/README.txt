Homepage hero images
=====================

Drop your two rotating hero photos here named exactly:
  hero-1.jpg
  hero-2.jpg

They become the background of the first two homepage hero slides. A dark
gradient overlay sits on top for text legibility, so choose images that are
clear on the left side (where the headline sits) — product shots, medical
packaging, or clinical scenes work best. Landscape, at least 1920x1000px.

If a file is missing the slide falls back to the current gradient — no error.
